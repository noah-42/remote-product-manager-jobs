Jobscout discovery and full details first, employer validation second. Worker-location restrictions, experience, language and pay are recorded separately. No hybrid or required weekly office attendance. Remote does not mean zero travel: company offsites are flagged. Europe does not mean every European country is permitted.

Excluded from this snapshot: junior/associate roles, product marketing, engineering management, region-unknown remote ads, and Coinbase's explicitly "remote-first, but not remote-only" role requiring quarterly in-person sessions. No applications, registrations or outreach made.
